# First Run: Find Your Way Around

Nova is a browser you and your connected AI program use together. This page covers the first few minutes: activate, connect, watch and stop.

## 1. Activating Nova

During the public alpha, no registration is needed. The setup and license sign-in window are prefilled with the shared alpha trial details. If Nova asks you to activate, use those details and choose **Sign in**. You can find them again on [Alpha Trial License](trial-license.md).

If you supplied your own email address and license key during setup, Nova uses them on first start. You can also leave the setup's optional activation fields empty and activate in the app later.

## 2. Connect your AI program

Nova may open **Welcome to Nova**, its connection wizard. Choose **Easy setup (recommended)**, review the programs it found, then continue to **How the connection is saved**. Click **Connect** if your program needs an entry; an already managed entry needs no further setup. Restart your AI program afterwards.

You can reopen the wizard at **Settings → AI & agents → Connection & setup → Set up**. Opening or paging through the wizard does not save a connection; its buttons do. More: [Connect an agent](../integration/README.md).

## 3. Where you are

The tab strip and address bar work like those of a familiar browser. Use `Ctrl+L` to enter an address and `Ctrl+T` for a new tab. Sandbox pills represent separate profiles, each with its own logins; you can explore those later.

Your conversation stays in your AI program. Ask it for a task there, and Nova is where you see its browser work. Start with the [first-task Quickstart](quickstart.md).

## 4. Where to see the agent

Tabs and sandbox pills show agent activity. Nova's optional **AI visualization** adds an AI cursor and captions while the agent interacts with pages. It is enabled by default; **Menu → View → AI visualization** switches it on or off.

## 5. How to stop or take over

Use **Menu → Emergency stop** to interrupt agent work. It stays active until you choose **Release emergency stop**. The stop also interrupts Nova's terminal sessions, including your own dock terminals.

To take over one reserved tab, select it and confirm **Take over** when Nova asks. Ordinary mouse or keyboard input does not pause an agent by itself. Details: [AI visualization and staying in control](../user-guide/live-assist-and-spectator.md).

## Explore when you need it

The [User guide](../user-guide/README.md) covers tabs, sandboxes, downloads, settings, permissions and the terminal dock. You can start your first task before learning these features.
