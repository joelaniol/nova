# Gemini research

Project-specific empirical studies of Gemini review outputs. Each study keeps its methods, dataset, figures and limitations together in its own folder; results should be interpreted within that project's workflow.

| Study | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Nova code-review reliability](nova-code-review/README.md) | 552 owner-attributed findings across 32 separately counterchecked catalogs; outcomes, context mismatches and project-growth timeline | Retrospective case study; 32 of 53 identified catalogs scored |

Additional project studies can be added alongside `nova-code-review/` as evidence becomes available. No cross-project or controlled model comparison has been established.
