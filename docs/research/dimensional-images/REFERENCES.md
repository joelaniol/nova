## 1. Peer-Reviewed Academic Literature

### Orthogonal Polynomials & Functional Analysis
1. **Gram, J. P. (1883).** *Ueber die Entwickelung reeller Functionen in Reihen mittelst der Methode der kleinsten Quadrate.* Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik, 94, 41–73. [DOI: 10.1515/crll.1883.94.41](https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1883.94.41)
   * *Significance:* Foundational mathematical formulation of discrete orthogonal polynomials on finite uniform grids (Gram polynomials).
2. **Chebyshev, P. L. (1875).** *Sur l'interpolation par la méthode des moindres carrés.* Mémoires de l'Académie Impériale des Sciences de Saint-Pétersbourg, 7(1), 1–24.
   * *Significance:* Discrete orthogonal polynomial systems and three-term recurrence formulations.
3. **Szegő, G. (1939).** *Orthogonal Polynomials.* American Mathematical Society Colloquium Publications, Vol. 23.
   * *Significance:* Canonical treatise on discrete and continuous orthogonal systems, weight functions, and recurrence properties.

### Signal Processing, Transforms & Information Theory
4. **Ahmed, N., Natarajan, T., & Rao, K. R. (1974).** *Discrete Cosine Transform.* IEEE Transactions on Computers, C-23(1), 90–93. [DOI: 10.1109/T-C.1974.223784](https://doi.org/10.1109/T-C.1974.223784)
   * *Significance:* The Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) and its energy compaction properties in spatial/temporal domain reduction.
5. **Mallat, S. (1999).** *A Wavelet Tour of Signal Processing.* Academic Press, 2nd Edition. ISBN: 978-0124666061.
   * *Significance:* Multiresolution signal representation, orthogonal wavelet decomposition, and time-frequency phase spaces.
6. **Daubechies, I. (1992).** *Ten Lectures on Wavelets.* CBMS-NSF Regional Conference Series in Applied Mathematics, SIAM. [DOI: 10.1137/1.9781611970104](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611970104)
   * *Significance:* Mathematical properties of compactly supported orthogonal wavelet bases.
7. **Shannon, C. E. (1948).** *A Mathematical Theory of Communication.* Bell System Technical Journal, 27(3), 379–423. [DOI: 10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x)
   * *Significance:* Channel capacity, entropy limits, and information-theoretic boundaries of lossy signal transmission.

### Vision Transformers & Multimodal Learning
8. **Dosovitskiy, A., Beyer, L., Kolesnikov, A., Weissenborn, D., Zhai, X., Unterthiner, T., Dehghani, M., Minderer, M., Heigold, G., Gelly, S., Uszkoreit, J., & Houlsby, N. (2020).** *An Image is Worth 16x16 Words: Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale.* International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2021). [arXiv:2010.11929](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11929)
   * *Significance:* Vision Transformer (ViT) architecture, patch tokenization, and self-attention dynamics over 2D visual grids.
9. **Radford, A., Kim, J. W., Hallacy, C., Ramesh, A., Goh, G., Agarwal, S., Sastry, G., Askell, A., Mishkin, P., Clark, J., Krueger, G., & Sutskever, I. (2021).** *Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision (CLIP).* International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2021). [arXiv:2103.00020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00020)
   * *Significance:* Contrastive language-image pre-training and multimodal semantic alignment.
