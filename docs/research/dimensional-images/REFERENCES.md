## 1. Discrete Orthogonal Polynomials & Functional Analysis

1. **Gram, J. P. (1883).** *Ueber die Entwickelung reeller Functionen in Reihen mittelst der Methode der kleinsten Quadrate.* Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik, 94, 41–73.
   * *Significance:* Foundational formulation of discrete orthogonal polynomials on finite uniform grids (Gram polynomials).
2. **Chebyshev, P. L. (1875).** *Sur l'interpolation par la méthode des moindres carrés.* Mémoires de l'Académie Impériale des Sciences de Saint-Pétersbourg.
   * *Significance:* Discrete Chebyshev/Gram polynomials and their recurrence relations.
3. **Szegő, G. (1939).** *Orthogonal Polynomials.* American Mathematical Society Colloquium Publications, Vol. 23.
   * *Significance:* Canonical reference on orthogonality, weight functions, and recurrence properties.

## 2. Signal Processing, Transforms & Compression

4. **Ahmed, N., Natarajan, T., & Rao, K. R. (1974).** *Discrete Cosine Transform.* IEEE Transactions on Computers, C-23(1), 90–93.
   * *Significance:* The Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) and spectral energy compaction in 2D image representations.
5. **Mallat, S. (1999).** *A Wavelet Tour of Signal Processing.* Academic Press, 2nd Edition.
   * *Significance:* Multiresolution signal representation, orthogonal wavelet bases, and time-frequency phase space analysis.
6. **Daubechies, I. (1992).** *Ten Lectures on Wavelets.* CBMS-NSF Regional Conference Series in Applied Mathematics, SIAM.
   * *Significance:* Compactly supported orthogonal wavelets and boundary handling.
7. **Shannon, C. E. (1948).** *A Mathematical Theory of Communication.* Bell System Technical Journal, 27(3), 379–423.
   * *Significance:* Channel capacity, source entropy, and fundamental limits of lossy signal reconstruction.

## 3. Multimodal Foundation Models & Vision Transformers

8. **Dosovitskiy, A., Beyer, L., Kolesnikov, A., Weissenborn, D., Zhai, X., Unterthiner, T., Dehghani, M., Minderer, M., Heigold, G., Gelly, S., Uszkoreit, J., & Houlsby, N. (2020).** *An Image is Worth 16x16 Words: Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale.* International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR).
   * *Significance:* Vision Transformer (ViT) patch tokenization and self-attention over 2D visual grids.
9. **Radford, A., Kim, J. W., Hallacy, C., Ramesh, A., Goh, G., Agarwal, S., Sastry, G., Askell, A., Mishkin, P., Clark, J., Krueger, G., & Sutskever, I. (2021).** *Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision (CLIP).* International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML).
   * *Significance:* Contrastive language-image pre-training and joint multimodal embedding spaces.
10. **OpenAI (2024).** *GPT-4o System Card and Technical Report.* OpenAI Research.
    * *Significance:* Omnimodal patch tokenization and high-density visual comprehension.
11. **Anthropic (2024).** *The Claude 3 Model Family: Opus, Sonnet, Haiku.* Anthropic Technical Documentation.
    * *Significance:* Multimodal prompt interpretation and fine-grained spatial image reasoning.
12. **Gemini Team, Google (2024).** *Gemini 1.5: Unlocking multimodal understanding across millions of tokens of context.* arXiv:2403.05530.
    * *Significance:* Video tokenization scaling, long-context window architecture, and temporal video comprehension.
