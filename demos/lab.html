Every case here breaks ordinary browser automation.

One page, eight kinds of trouble: state that must survive, a list that only exists while you look at it, elements hidden behind shadow roots and frames, gestures instead of clicks, files going both ways, dialogs the page cannot see, and an overlay that eats every click until it is answered.
